Morder villagers, women included, train to face floods and landslides before they strike

In a valley that has seen glacial floods, cloudbursts and landslides in recent years, the village of Morder in Upper Chitral has been learning how to be ready for the next one.

The Sarhad Rural Support Programme held a disaster risk reduction and early warning training in the village, with community members, including women, taking part.

The sessions covered the hazards people in the area face, how to spot which homes and families are most at risk, how to evacuate, where to find safe shelter and basic first aid. Participants also learned how village response committees should coordinate when an emergency hits.

The training is part of the PEACE-II project, funded by the European Union and run by SRSP. The aim is simple: when an early warning comes, people should know exactly what to do to protect their families and their livelihoods.
