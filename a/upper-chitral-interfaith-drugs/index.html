Upper Chitral leaders pledge harmony as a district-wide drug crackdown runs to 30 November

Officials and religious leaders met in Booni this week to recommit to the peace between communities that Upper Chitral is known for, and to back a major push against drugs.

The meeting was chaired by Deputy Commissioner Arshad Khan and attended by senior officials, the Tehsil Khateeb and the president of the Ismaili Council for Upper Chitral. Participants praised the mutual respect between the district's two religious communities and agreed to keep working together.

Police told the meeting that a large anti-drug operation is under way across the district and will continue until 30 November 2026.

The deputy commissioner said religious harmony in the district was in good shape, and that beating drug abuse would take strict enforcement, cooperation between departments and public awareness. The meeting ended with a pledge to work together for a drug-free, peaceful Upper Chitral.
