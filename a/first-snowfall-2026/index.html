Winter arrives early: Shandur, Broghil and Madaklasht get the season's first snow

Upper Chitral woke up to its first snowfall of the 2026-27 winter, and temperatures dropped sharply across the region.

Snow fell at Shandur Top, Khot, Broghil, Madaklasht and other high areas, according to the local administration. The Met Office forecast more on-and-off snow over the following two days.

For remote villages, the first snow is a warning sign. Supplies become harder to bring in, and high mountain roads turn slippery and risky.

Officials advised residents to stock warm clothes, fuel and household essentials, and asked tourists to check the weather and road updates before travelling and avoid unnecessary trips.
