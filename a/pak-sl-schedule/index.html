Pakistan vs Sri Lanka Tests: venues, dates and start times

Pakistan host Sri Lanka in a two-Test series this month. The first Test is at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium from 9 November, and the second at Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore, from 17 November.

Play starts at 9:30 am Pakistan time each day.

Pakistan come in on a high after winning the T20I series and an ODI tri-series, but their Test record in the current World Test Championship cycle is two wins and seven defeats. Sri Lanka have one win, two losses and three draws from six.

Sri Lanka's Pathum Nissanka returns to the side, while Pakistan have left out Shan Masood, Noman Ali and Sajid Khan.
