"Yomoon Ki Hoi": the great Chitrali winter exodus, told with a smile

Every November the same thing happens. The snow arrives, and a big part of Chitral packs up for Peshawar, Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi, for work, studies, medical care or simply to get away from the cold.

A new feature and Khowar-language video, Yomoon Ki Hoi Chetrari Goi, has fun with this yearly migration. It jokes that city households somehow develop sudden emergencies just as the Shandur festival approaches, so their Chitrali staff can rush back north.

Then spring comes, the snow melts, the valley turns green, and everyone returns home.

The piece ends on a line many Chitralis will recognise: you can leave Chitral for the winter, but part of you always stays behind.
