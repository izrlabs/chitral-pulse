Ex-MNA Shahzada Iftikhar says timber mafia threatened to kill him over Rs135m royalties

Former MNA and PML-N Chitral leader Shahzada Iftikharuddin says he has received death threats from a man calling himself Shah Zarin of Barawal in Upper Dir.

He says the threats began after he backed the people of Birga village in Shishikoh, who say timber businesses owe them around Rs135 million in forest royalties. According to him, the same man had earlier threatened a schoolteacher, Sajjad, and his brothers over complaints they took to the courts and NAB.

He pointed to a Drosh tehsildar report showing that only eight residents supported the Forest Department's new marking plan, while 992 opposed it. He also questioned a Forest Development Corporation contract worth more than Rs2 billion for about 170,000 cubic feet of timber, awarded to Qari Shoaib despite local objections.

Iftikharuddin has appealed to Field Marshal Asim Munir, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi and the NAB chairman for protection and an investigation. Former provincial minister Saleem Khan and other leaders have backed his call for an urgent probe. No official response had been reported.
