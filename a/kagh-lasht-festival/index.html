Polo, football and crowds: the Kagh Lasht festival wraps up in Upper Chitral

The week-long Kagh Lasht festival on the high plain above Booni came to a close with big crowds and plenty of sport.

Organised by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Culture and Tourism Authority, the festival featured polo, football, cricket, volleyball and traditional games. Jughoor beat Tiger club to win the football final.

Upper Chitral Deputy Commissioner Haseebur Rehman Khan Khalil, the chief guest, thanked the organisers, police, Levies and Rescue 1122. He said sport helps keep young people away from drugs, and that Kagh Lasht draws tourists and brings business to the area.

Trophies and prizes were handed out at the closing ceremony.
