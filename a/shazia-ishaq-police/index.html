Shazia Ishaq: Chitral's first woman police officer

Shazia Ishaq from Junali Koch in Upper Chitral made history when she passed the CSS exam and was selected for the Police Service of Pakistan, becoming the first woman police officer from Chitral and from the whole Malakand division.

A graduate of Islamia College University, Peshawar, and the daughter of a retired army officer, she was set to train as an Assistant Superintendent of Police.

She said she wants to help women in Chitral, where abuse often goes unreported because of stigma, and called domestic violence a serious concern in the district.

A retired police officer described her appointment as a ray of hope. Women make up a tiny share of Pakistan's police, so her success matters well beyond Chitral.
