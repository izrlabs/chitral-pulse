We make the power, so why pay Rs28 a unit? Reshun threatens to block Shandur Road

Residents of Reshun village in Upper Chitral have given the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government five days to keep its promise of cheaper electricity, or they will block the Shandur Road.

The village gets its power from a 4.2 megawatt hydropower station built by the provincial energy department. People say they were promised a rate of Rs7 a unit, but are being charged Rs28.

At a public meeting at Ramdas Chowk, chaired by Syed Sardar Hussain, speakers including former union nazim Amirullah said they felt betrayed by their MPA, KP Assembly Deputy Speaker Suraiya Bibi. They had called off a protest 40 days earlier, during the Shandur festival, after she gave them a written assurance.

Blocking the Shandur Road would cut the link between Upper Chitral, Lower Chitral and Gilgit-Baltistan. Dawn reported that the deputy speaker could not be reached for comment.
