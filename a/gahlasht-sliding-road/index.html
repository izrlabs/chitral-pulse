Falling rocks hit a family car and a school van near Gahlasht Bridge. Now the NHA is being asked to act

A dangerous sliding stretch of the Chitral-Mastuj road near Gahlasht Bridge is finally getting attention after two frightening incidents.

A few days ago, stones came down on a car carrying a family, including women. They jumped out and ran to safety, leaving the car behind. The same morning a falling stone reportedly struck a van full of schoolchildren. Thankfully, none of the students was hurt.

Upper Chitral Deputy Commissioner Arshad Khan called a meeting with officials and NESPAK project manager Ibrahim Khan, who explained the technical and funding problems at the site. The deputy commissioner then contacted the National Highway Authority and pressed for urgent work.

The plan is for an NHA consultant to assess the slope first, followed by a retaining wall, filling work and a temporary diversion road. Machinery is on standby, and Levies and police will manage traffic in the meantime. No completion date has been given, so drivers should take extra care on this stretch.
