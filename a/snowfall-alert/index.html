Heavy snow warning for Chitral: PDMA puts eight districts on winter alert

Get ready for a hard winter. The Provincial Disaster Management Authority of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has issued a winter emergency alert for eight upper districts, including Upper and Lower Chitral.

The alert also covers Upper and Lower Dir, Buner, Swat, and Upper and Lower Kohistan. It warns of above-normal rain and heavy snowfall this season, which could close mountain roads and disrupt transport.

The PDMA says its winter plan is in place, relief supplies have been arranged in advance for snow-hit areas, and all departments have been told to stay on alert for the whole season.

For families in Chitral, the advice is the usual but important one: stock up on fuel, food and medicines early, and check road conditions before any journey up the valley.
