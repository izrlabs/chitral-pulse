Gold medals to Europe: Barenis' Faiza Qadeer wins a Marie Curie PhD scholarship

Faiza Qadeer from Barenis Lasht in Lower Chitral has won a fully funded Marie Sklodowska-Curie scholarship, one of Europe's most competitive research awards, to do a PhD at the Universita Cattolica del Sacro Cuore in Piacenza, Italy.

She studied at Public School and College Jutial in Gilgit and FG Degree College for Women in Peshawar Cantt, then earned a BS in Agriculture and an MS in Plant Pathology at the University of Agriculture, Peshawar, taking gold medals in both.

She thanked her teachers and her parents' prayers, and urged girls to keep working steadily towards their goals.

Education runs in the family: her sister Sabahat Ayesha, a civil engineer, is doing an MS in Water Resource Management, and her sister Zopash Qadeer has a BS in Statistics.
