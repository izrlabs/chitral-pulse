Three new faces as Pakistan add four players for the Sri Lanka Tests

The Pakistan Cricket Board has added four players to the squad for the two-Test series against Sri Lanka, three of them earning their first international call-ups.

Pace all-rounder Aamer Jamal returns, joined by fast bowlers Ali Shabbir and Saqib Khan and batter Muhammad Tayyab Arif. Ali Usman and Abdullah Fazal were released.

The newcomers earned their places with big domestic seasons. Tayyab scored 727 runs in seven matches with three centuries, Saqib took 47 wickets in nine Quaid-e-Azam Trophy games, and Jamal took 7 for 32 in the President's Trophy final.

Babar Azam leads the side. The series opened in Rawalpindi on 9 November.
