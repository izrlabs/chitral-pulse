17 women, one mountain: the first all-female team reaches Tirich Mir advance base camp

For the first time, an all-women trekking team has reached the advance base camp of Tirich Mir, at 7,708 metres the highest peak in the Hindu Kush.

The 17 women crossed glaciers and high passes and went beyond Babu Base Camp, a point only men's teams had reached before. The group included a Kalasha woman, three women from Chitral, four from Gilgit-Baltistan and others from Balochistan and Islamabad, supported by 36 local porters and three guides.

Their route ran from Chitral through Shughrum, Shenyak Camp, Byasam and Astor Nall. The youngest member of the expedition was six-year-old Shiz Mir, the son of a local guide, on his very first trek.

The trek was organised by the KP Culture and Tourism Authority and the Tirich Mir Backpackers Club. The province has named 2025-26 the Year of Tirich Mir, and officials hope the achievement will bring more women and international climbers to Chitral.
