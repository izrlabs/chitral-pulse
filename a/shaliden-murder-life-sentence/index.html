It was called a suicide. A court says it was murder: four get life, three of them women

A court in Lower Chitral has sentenced four people, three of them women, to life in prison for the murder of Zahirul Haq of Shaliden in May 2024.

His death was first presented as a suicide. His brother, Tahir Khan, did not accept that and filed a case at Ayun Police Station. Additional Sessions Judge Adam Khan Sulaiman Khel has now convicted Noreena Bibi, Salam Bibi, Saeed Hakeem Khan and Gul Nisa, all relatives of the victim's in-laws, under Sections 302/34.

Each must also pay Rs200,000 to the victim's heirs, or serve six more months if they do not. Their bail was cancelled and they are being moved to Central Jail Chitral.

District Public Prosecutor Qaid-ul-Islam argued for the state, and the family was represented by lawyers Hazratuddin, Sirajuddin Rabbani and Rizwanullah. For the family, it ends a two-and-a-half-year fight to have the truth recognised.
