Village giant-killers: Zang Bazar FC beat Army and Navy to win the Chitral Football League

In their very first season, Zang Bazar FC have won the Chitral Football League in Drosh, knocking out two of the country's strongest department teams on the way.

Fifteen teams took part, six of them departmental sides. Zang Bazar lost 5-2 to Pakistan Navy in the group stage, then beat HQ Jughoor and Pakistan Army on penalties in the quarter-final and semi-final.

The final was a rematch with Navy. After 120 minutes it was 0-0, and Zang Bazar held their nerve to win 4-2 on penalties. Umer Sadozai from AJK was named player of the tournament, the first foreign player to feature in a Chitral sports event.

The winners took home Rs300,000 and were cheered by a five-hour cavalcade from Drosh to Chitral town. Club president Bilal Mumtaz said the win effectively made them national-level champions. It is the first league title ever won by a village club.
