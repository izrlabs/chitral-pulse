Suriya Bibi: the first Chitrali woman to win a provincial seat by direct vote

In the 2024 general election, Suriya Bibi became the first woman from Chitral district to win a seat in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Assembly through a direct election, rather than a reserved seat.

It was a milestone for a district where women have rarely contested, let alone won, general seats.

She has since become Deputy Speaker of the KP Assembly, and as the local MPA she now carries the hopes, and the complaints, of her constituents on issues such as electricity tariffs and roads.
