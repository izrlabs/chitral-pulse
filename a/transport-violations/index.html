Overloaded coasters, extra seats, inflated fares: 18 months of warnings and Chitral transport still breaks the rules

Anyone who has travelled between Chitral and Peshawar knows the problem: packed coasters, folding seats squeezed into the aisle, reckless driving and fares well above the official rate. Despite a year and a half of official warnings, little has changed.

On 16 November the Regional Transport Authority for Malakand Division wrote again to the deputy commissioners and police of Upper and Lower Chitral, telling them to take legal action and impound vehicles that break the rules, with special attention to overloading at night.

It is not the first letter. Similar orders went out in June and July this year after complaints from local journalists and passengers. In October, an inspection found a vehicle on the route with no route permit, no fitness certificate and a driver without a valid licence. The provincial transport authority has also told bus stand operators to follow the law.

The rules exist under the Motor Vehicles Ordinance of 1965. What passengers want now is action they can see, and public reports showing which vehicles have actually been checked and fined.
