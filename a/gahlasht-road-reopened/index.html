Landslide cleared: road near Gahlasht Bridge open to traffic again

Traffic is moving again on the road near Gahlasht Bridge in Upper Chitral after a landslide blocked it.

The district administration said the National Highway Authority cleared the rocks and debris on the instructions of Deputy Commissioner Arshad Khan.

The slide came days after another landslide blocked the Chitral-Mastuj road at Karak. Both point to how fragile this route is as winter approaches.

Drivers should stay alert on this stretch, which remains one of the most dangerous on the road to Mastuj.
