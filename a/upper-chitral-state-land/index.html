Rs145 million in state land taken back from encroachers in Upper Chitral

The Upper Chitral administration says it recovered 215 kanals of government land in October, worth about Rs145.3 million, in a drive against encroachment.

Most of it, 200 kanals worth around Rs128.8 million, was at Qaqlasht, the famous plain above Booni where the spring festival is held. Another 15 kanals, worth about Rs16.5 million, were recovered at Awi.

The monthly report also lists a facelift for Booni with new streetlights, wall paintings and matching shop signboards, an open court where residents could raise complaints directly, and work to widen and blacktop the Charun-Booni bridge road.

A Jirga Hall is being built at the deputy commissioner's office, and plans are moving ahead for Sorankham Public Park with gardens, walking tracks and play areas for children.
