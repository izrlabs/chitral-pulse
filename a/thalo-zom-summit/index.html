No porters, no fixed ropes: Chitrali team climbs 6,050 m Thalo Zom

A nine-member team of local climbers from Chitral, called Shabdaraz, reached the summit of Thalo Zom, a 6,050-metre peak in the Hindu Raj range, at 9:20 am on 29 July. Everyone returned safely.

Led by Mueezuddin, the team climbed alpine style, with no fixed ropes, no porters and no pre-set camps. They went up the Italian Route, first climbed in 2019, and came down a different, unused line. The peak was first climbed by an Austrian team in 1971.

The expedition took 11 days and cost up to Rs1.5 million, with gear from the Hindukush Snow Sports Club and Zom Connection.

Team member Athar Azeem called it a milestone for local mountaineering and a model for expeditions led by young Chitralis.
