From a Booni classroom to a PhD: Parwak educator Shafiqur Rehman makes history

Shafiqur Rehman, an educator from Parwak village in Upper Chitral, has earned a PhD in Educational Planning, Policy Studies and Leadership from Allama Iqbal Open University. He is the first person from the Parwak, Sonoghur and Miragram No 1 area to earn a doctorate.

His research looked at how universities in Punjab and Sindh are governed, and was supervised by Prof Dr Afshan Huma.

His journey started close to home. After an MSc in Chemistry from Peshawar in 2003, he taught chemistry at Pearl Degree College for Women in Booni until 2007. He then spent ten years with the Aga Khan Education Service in Chitral as a lecturer, academic coordinator and field education officer, while adding an MA from AIOU, an MEd from Aga Khan University and later an MPhil.

In 2017 he joined the Federal Directorate of Education in Islamabad as a headmaster, rose to vice principal, and since 2023 has served as an area education officer.

Messages of congratulations have poured in from Chitral, including from the Chitral Press Club. For young people in Parwak, his story is proof that the road from a village school can lead all the way to a doctorate.
