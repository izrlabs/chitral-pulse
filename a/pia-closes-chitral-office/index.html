Last plane has left the building: PIA shuts its Chitral office for good

Pakistan International Airlines has permanently closed its office in Chitral and taken its remaining equipment out of Chitral Airport, local sources say.

The move comes after PIA was privatised. Its Islamabad-Chitral flights had already been suspended for years, but as long as the office stayed open, many people held on to the hope that the route would one day come back. That hope now looks a lot thinner.

No official reason has been given for the closure.

For a remote mountain district, the loss is more than symbolic. In winter, when snow and landslides close the roads, a short flight was often the only quick way in or out for patients, students and families. Chitralis will now be asking their representatives one question: who will bring the planes back?
