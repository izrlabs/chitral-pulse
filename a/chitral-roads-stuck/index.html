Ten years and still waiting: why Chitral’s key roads are stuck

Ask anyone in Chitral what their biggest problem is, and the answer is usually the same: roads.

Shabbir Ahmed Khan, chairman of the Chitral Development Forum, points to three projects. The 153 km Chitral-Shandur Road and the 46 km Kalash Valleys Road have both been stuck for around ten years because of technical and funding problems. The 35 km Booni-Buzund Road started in 2008 with a 2012 deadline, and is still not finished.

He also criticised the National Highway Authority over the road from the Lowari Tunnel to Chitral town, where pothole patches did not last a month. He said a promise of faster work made during the Prime Minister's visit last October has produced nothing.

Bad roads push up the cost of living, make it harder to reach hospitals and keep tourists away. With 36 side valleys that need year-round access, the forum says it will launch a valley-wide movement to push the government into action.
