Chitrali embroidery on the Milan runway: Karishma Ali's big moment

Karishma Ali from Chitral helped Italian-Haitian designer Stella Jean create a collection shown at Milan Fashion Week, inspired by embroidery from Chitral, Kalash and Hunza.

Her organisation, the Chitral Women's Handicrafts Center, produced 400 metres of hand embroidery for the collection, working with women artisans from ten villages.

Karishma is also the first woman from her hometown to play for Pakistan's national women's football team, was named on a Forbes Asia list of influential young people, and in 2018 founded Chitral's first women's sports club.

She said the aim was simple: to empower local women through their own craft.
