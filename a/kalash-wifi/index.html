The Kalash valleys go online: satellite phone and Wi-Fi arrive in Bumburet

Bumburet, the largest of the Kalash valleys, now has satellite phone and Wi-Fi service, set up by the Kalash Valley Development Authority.

The system runs from camping pods, and residents were given the first month free. The authority says the service will be extended across the valley.

KVDA Director General Minhasuddin said the connection will help residents and let tourists keep in touch with their families while they share the valley's culture and scenery with the world. Board member Sartaj Ahmed was credited with bringing the facility to the valley.

For guesthouses that depend on bookings, and for students who have struggled to get online, it is a welcome change.
