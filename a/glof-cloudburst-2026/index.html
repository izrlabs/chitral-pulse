Chitral cut off: glacial flood and cloudburst wash away bridges, roads and internet

A glacial lake outburst flood and a cloudburst, followed by a sudden rise in temperature, caused heavy damage across Chitral this summer, cutting roads, bridges, water pipelines and the main fibre-optic cable. Internet service went down.

The damage stretched from Sheshi Koh Valley and Drosh in Lower Chitral to Mastuj in Upper Chitral. Both the Chitral-Peshawar Highway and the Chitral-Shandur Road were closed, leaving Upper Chitral cut off from the rest of the country.

A cloudburst over Broz sent flash floods through Gumbas Gol and Broz Gol. At least two people died, including Mukhtar Wali Khan of Broz, and Rescue 1122 recovered an unidentified body from the Chitral River. Nearly a dozen bridges were destroyed in Karimabad Valley.

At Parwak, landslides blocked the Booni-Mastuj road and trapped more than 150 vehicles for 16 hours. Stranded travellers complained that relief was slow and that tractors were used instead of heavy machinery.

Lower Chitral's deputy commissioner confirmed relief teams and a damage survey were under way. The NHA began building temporary bridge diversions at Broz Gol, and groups such as Focus Humanitarian Pakistan were helping on the ground. Clean drinking water was the biggest need.
