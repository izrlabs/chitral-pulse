Step inside an old Chitrali house, the kind our grandparents grew up in

A short reel doing the rounds on Facebook this week takes viewers inside a traditional old-style house in Chitral, and for many Chitralis it is a trip straight back to childhood.

Houses like this were built from what the valley gave: thick walls of stone and timber, and a flat roof of wooden beams packed with earth. The heavy walls keep the cold out in winter and the rooms cool in summer.

Life happened in one big main room. Families sat, ate, slept and welcomed guests there, around a hearth in the middle, with light and fresh air coming in through an opening in the roof. Carved wooden pillars and beams were a point of pride, and raised platforms along the walls served as seats by day and beds by night.

Today concrete is taking over across the district, and fewer of these homes are left standing. That is exactly why clips like this get shared so widely: they show a way of living that many families still remember, and that younger Chitralis may only ever see on a screen.
